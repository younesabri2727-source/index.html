Privacy at a glance

We do not directly collect personal information or require user accounts. The game displays advertisements, and third-party ad providers may process technical information.

1. Information Collection

We do not directly collect, store, or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Our game does not require account registration. Please note that third-party advertising services may independently process information as described below.

2. Advertising

Our game displays third-party advertisements to support its development and maintenance. Depending on the advertising service used, advertising providers may automatically process:

  • Device information and advertising identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Ad interactions, diagnostics, and performance data

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies and applicable privacy laws. For information about Google's data practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy ↗.

3. Third-Party Services

Our game may use third-party advertising services. Such services operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies to understand how they process information.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Third-party advertising providers may process technical information in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws. If you have a concern about a child's privacy, please contact us.

5. Data Security

We do not maintain a database of players' personal information. Any information processed by third-party providers is subject to their respective security and data handling practices.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
